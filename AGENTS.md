### Case Study: Quantum Tech Corporate Espionage

**Incident:**
> "Quantum Tech's IT security team detected unauthorized access to their proprietary patent database, specifically the AI chip design patent (ID: QT-AI-2025). Initial findings point to Emma, a senior engineer, joined recently at Quantum Tech's as the primary suspect. The company suspects Emma attempted to steal and sell these patents to a competitor, NexGen Circuits, through an intermediary, Joe."

**Suspects:**
- **Emma:** Senior engineer, primary suspect (PERPETRATOR)
  - Recently joined Quantum Tech
  - Attempted to steal and sell patents
  
- **Joe:** Intermediary
  - Facilitating transfer to competitor
  
- **Vanessa:** R&D Director at NexGen Circuits (BUYER)
  - Also referred to as "Norman" in some contexts
  - Suspected buyer of stolen patents

**Evidence Provided (Seized under continuity-of-evidence protocols):**

1. **Emma's Android Smartphone**
   - WhatsApp communication logs
   - Instagram DMs
   - Emails
   - Call records

2. **Emma's USB Flash Drive**
   - Files related to the QT-AI-2025 patent

3. **Joe's Smartphone**
   - WhatsApp communication logs
   - Instagram DMs
   - Call records

4. **Norman's Android Smartphone** (Note: Vanessa/Norman same person)
   - WhatsApp communication logs
   - Emails
   - Call records

**Legal Context:**
- Reported to law enforcement
- Findings will support legal action
- Charges: Corporate espionage, theft, potentially other crimes

### Required Tasks (Complete Investigation)

#### Task 2.1: Evidence Acquisition
- Acquire forensic images of:
  - Emma's smartphone
  - Vanessa's (Norman's) smartphone
  - Joe's smartphone
  - Emma's USB flash drive
- **Tools:** Cellebrite, FTK Imager, or Autopsy
- Document continuity of evidence for each device
- Ensure integrity and admissibility of evidence
- Verify hashes (MD5, SHA-1) of acquired images
- Confirm no tampering

#### Task 2.2: Communication Analysis
- Extract communication logs from:
  - WhatsApp (all 3 smartphones)
  - Instagram DMs (all 3 smartphones)
  - Emails (Emma's and Vanessa's smartphones)
- Reconstruct timeline of interactions between Emma, Joe, and Norman
- Identify key discussions related to:
  - Theft and sale of QT-AI-2025 patent
  - Payment mentions
  - Meeting arrangements
  - File transfers

#### Task 2.3: File Analysis
- Examine Emma's USB flash drive contents
- Identify files related to QT-AI-2025 patent:
  - PDFs
  - CAD designs
- Investigate additional files that may constitute other evidences
- Analyse file metadata:
  - Creation dates
  - Modification dates
  - Determine when and how files were created or copied

#### Task 2.4: Call Log and Email Correlation
- Cross-reference call logs from all three smartphones
- Confirm communication events
- Specifically identify key call between Emma, Joe and Vanessa
- Analyse email exchanges involving patent sample transfers
- Verify intent to sell proprietary data

#### Task 2.5: Suspect Profiling
- Determine roles and motives:
  - Emma (perpetrator)
  - Joe (intermediary)
  - Norman (buyer)
- Identify additional evidence supporting or refuting involvement in corporate fraud

#### Task 2.6: Forensic Images Submission
- Submit acquired images or documentation
- Devices: Emma (perpetrator), Joe (intermediary), Norman (buyer)

**Required Sections:**

1. **Case Background**

2. **Evidence Inventory**
   - List of devices

6. **Recommendations**
